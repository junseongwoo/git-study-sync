STEP 4-2. ROLLUP 함수 일부 사용
[예제] GROUP BY ROLLUP (DNAME, JOB) 조건에서 GROUP BY DNAME, ROLLUP(JOB) 조건으로 변경한 경우다.

SELECT CASE GROUPING (B.DNAME) WHEN 1 THEN ‘All Departments’ ELSE B.DNAME END AS DNAME
, CASE GROUPING (A.JOB ) WHEN 1 THEN ‘All Jobs’       ELSE A.J0B END AS 208

, COUNT (*) AS EMP_CNT, SUM (A.SAL) AS SAL_SUM
FROM EMP A, DEPT B
WHERE B.DEPTNO = A,DEPTNO
GROUP BY B.DNAME, ROLLUP (A.J0B)
ORDER BY B.DNAME, A.J0B;
